This procedure guides staff in responding to requests from customers wishing to exercise their data rights under Bahrain's data protection framework. It applies to all customer-facing staff at the Bahrain branches.

## 1. Types of Customer Rights Requests

Customers may make the following types of requests regarding their personal data:

- Access Request — customer asks what data BBK holds about them
- Correction Request — customer asks for inaccurate data to be corrected
- Deletion Request — customer asks for their data to be deleted
- Restriction Request — customer asks to limit how their data is used

## 2. Receiving a Request

Requests may be received in person at a branch, by email, or by post. When a request is received, staff should:

- Note the date and type of request.
- Pass the request to the branch compliance contact within 3 business days.
- Complete Form BBK-DSR-FORM if required. For simple requests this step is optional.

## 3. Responding to an Access Request

When a customer requests access to their personal data, the branch compliance contact should:

- Locate all personal data held for that customer across relevant systems.
- Compile a summary of the data and the purposes for which it is processed.
- Send the response to the customer within 30 days of receiving the request.

If more time is needed, the branch may take up to an additional 30 days. The customer does not need to be informed of this extension.

## 4. Responding to a Correction Request

If a customer believes their data is inaccurate, they may ask for it to be corrected. Staff should:

- Review the data in question.
- Make the correction in the relevant system if the request is valid.
- Confirm the correction to the customer in writing.

## 5. Responding to a Deletion Request

Customers may request deletion of their personal data. BBK will try to action deletion requests within 60 days.

Deletion requests may be refused if the data is required to be kept for legal or regulatory reasons, such as AML obligations. In such cases, the customer will be informed.

## 6. Restriction Requests

Where a customer asks to restrict the processing of their data, the branch should flag the relevant account and refer the matter to compliance. Further guidance will be provided on a case-by-case basis.

## 7. Keeping Records

Branch compliance contacts should keep a record of requests handled. The BBK-DSR-FORM log is available for this purpose and is recommended for use in all cases.

## 8. Escalation

Complex or unusual requests should be escalated to the Head of Compliance. Where a customer is dissatisfied with BBK's response they may contact the relevant regulator.
