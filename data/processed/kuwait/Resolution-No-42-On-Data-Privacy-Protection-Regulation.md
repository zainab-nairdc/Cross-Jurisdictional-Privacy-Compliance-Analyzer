
## No. (42) of 2021

## On Data Privacy Protection Regulation

## Chairman of the Board:-

- After reviewing Law No. 37 of 2014 establishing the COMMUNICATION & INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY REGULATORY AUTHORITY, as amended by Law No. 98 of 2015.
- Decree No. 312 of 2018 forming the Board of Directors of the COMMUNICATION & INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY REGULATORY AUTHORITY.
- Cabinet Resolution No. 993 of 2015 issued on 7/13/2015 issuing the executive regulations of the above stated Law No. 37 of 2014.
- Decree No. 312 of 2018 renewing the formation of the Board of Directors of the COMMUNICATION & INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY REGULATORY AUTHORITY.
- The approval of the Board of the COMMUNICATION & INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY REGULATORY AUTHORITY at its meeting 4/2021 No. (4/2021) dated 3/28/2021,
- Based on what is required by the work interest,

## It Decided

## (Article I)

The provisions of the regulations attached to this decision (the Data Privacy Protection Regulation) shall apply to the public and private sectors in the State of Kuwait.

## (Article II)

The competent authorities - each within its purview- shall apply this decision, and it shall be effective from the date of its publication in the Official Gazette and the official website of the COMMUNICATION & INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY REGULATORY AUTHORITY.

## (Article III)

Any text or provision shall be considered void when in contradiction or contravention with this decision.
